SAM HESKETH

Hi, I'm Sam.

I'm a photographer, filmmaker, and creative director with a foundation in architecture and a life lived on the road. Since 2015 I've been shooting stills and video, brand films, short docs, product stories, and campaign work across coastlines, studios, alpine country, and the outback. I work globally, but I grew up between Tasmania and the UK; that mix of places shaped my eye, curious, grounded, and open to the wild.

Design taught me to build with intention; years behind the lens taught me light, timing, and human connection. Adventure keeps me curious and adaptive. Together, they shape how I tell stories, structured, human, and alive. I care about work that's beautiful, tangible, and well-crafted.

MY PHILOSOPHY

Make it true. Make it loved. Make it last.

I make work people can feel. The product should look incredible, but the heart of it is a story worth caring about. I'm a bit of a purist, simple, honest, on-mission. I'll chase big ideas, but only when they belong to the brand and serve its purpose. If we earn real connection, the rest follows. Help people fall in love with the brand, and the product will do the talking.

CREATIVE DIRECTION & STORYCRAFT

Ideas with a backbone, and a heartbeat

I turn the why, what, and how into a clear plan: concept, look/feel, and a narrative arc that serves the brand and the brief. My approach is built on years behind the lens in both stills and video, shaped by travel and a solid creative process, so the story stays honest and the execution stays sharp. I'm comfortable in the uncomfortable, tight timelines, tricky terrain, messy realities, if it gets us to the best possible outcome. From treatments and mood to shot design, pacing, and sound, I steer the work from first scribble to final cut.

CAPABILITY FOR REMOTE PROJECTS

Light footprint, steady hands.

Remote work is part of how I operate. I plan well, pack light, and stay flexible when weather or access shifts. Years in the field taught me to get the shot with what we've got, natural light, simple rigs, local resources, instead of hauling a truckload of gear. I'm comfortable travelling solo or with a small crew and taking the long way in when the story calls for it. Improvisation is built in: rethink the shot, reroute the day, repurpose a headlamp or a tarp if that's the move. I keep the footprint small, comms clear, and safety front and centre, working respectfully with local people so the work stays honest and the place stays looked after.

Passage

WHERE IT
ALL STARTED

  1. 2007.

    UNIVERSITY OF TASMANIA, SCHOOL OF ARCHITECTURE, 2007.

    I studied Architecture at the University of Tasmania, where I learnt to think beyond form and function, to build concepts grounded in deep values and narrative.

    That training shaped how I approach creative work today: every project begins with intention, a clear idea, and a story worth carrying. Architecture gave me more than design skills, it taught me to see structure in story, and to weave meaning into the spaces brands inhabit.

  2. 2010.

    H2O ARCHITECTS, MELBOURNE AUSTRALIA, 2010.

    My time at H2O Architects was my first real step into the commercial design world working alongside creatives navigating complex, real world projects with budgets exceeding $100 million. It was a crash course in high level collaboration, from corporate meetings and workflows to the daily rhythm of a tight, healthy team of twelve experienced project and senior architects. I learnt how big ideas come to life through teamwork, clarity, and creative discipline.

  3. 2013.

    SNOWMAKER, ROCKY MOUNTAINS, CANADA, 2013

    Working as a snowmaker at Lake Louise Ski Resort in Canada was a season of grit and camaraderie. Nights spent in -40°C, hiking through knee deep snow with a headlamp, snowboard, and a flask of hot soup taught me what endurance really feels like. Our crew was small but solid, comrades bound by the cold and a shared love for the mountains. That time deepened my connection to the outdoors and gave me a fire in the belly that still drives the way I approach every adventure and project today.

  4. 2015.

    SAM HESKETH CREATIVE, PRODUCTION, 2021

    2015 marked the beginning of my freelance journey, a turning point in my work, and in many ways my life. Stepping out on my own meant saying yes to uncertainty, learning to build something from the ground up. I chased every job I could, learning to balance creativity with survival, art with business. Those early years taught me to trust my own path, stay adaptable, and let persistence and passion keep the wheels turning.
