PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated 15 September 2026Samuel Hesketh, trading as Sam Hesketh CreativeAustralia

This policy explains what personal information Sam Hesketh Creative collects when you visit this website, get in touch, sign up to the Project Log or order from the General Store, and how that information is handled. It is written in plain English on purpose. If anything is unclear, ask.

Who we are What we collect How we collect it Why we use it Who we share it with Overseas disclosure Cookies and embedded media People in our pictures Keeping it safe How long we keep it Access and correction Complaints Changes Contact
01

Who we are

Sam Hesketh Creative is the trading name of Samuel Hesketh, a sole trader based in Australia who works as a photographer, filmmaker and creative director. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Samuel Hesketh trading as Sam Hesketh Creative, and "you" means anyone who visits this website or deals with us.

As a small business we may not be legally required to comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We choose to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles regardless, because it is the right way to treat the people we work with.

02

What we collect

We only collect what we need to answer you, deliver a project or fill an order. Depending on how you deal with us, that can include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • The content of messages you send through the contact form, by email, phone or WhatsApp, including details about your project, brand and budget.
  • Your email address when you sign up to the Project Log.
  • Your postal address, delivery instructions and order history when you buy from the General Store.
  • Business details such as your company name, role and billing address when we quote or invoice you.
  • Technical information sent by your browser, such as your IP address, device and browser type and the pages you visit, held in our hosting provider's standard server logs.

We do not ask for sensitive information such as health details or government identifiers. If a project genuinely requires it, for example a medical declaration before an expedition, we will explain why and how it will be handled before you provide it.

03

How we collect it

Most of the information we hold comes directly from you: when you fill in the contact or Project Log forms, email or call us, book a project or place an order. Forms on this site are delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service, so your submission passes through their servers on the way to our inbox.

Some information is collected automatically by our web host's server logs when you view the site. Occasionally we receive personal information from third parties, for example when a client or agency introduces us to talent, crew or a supplier, or when a courier provides delivery details.

04

Why we use it

We use personal information:

  • To respond to enquiries and quote on projects.
  • To plan, shoot, produce and deliver commissioned work, and to manage the crew, talent and logistics that go with it.
  • To send the Project Log, our occasional email dispatch about expeditions, new work and the General Store. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
  • To process General Store orders, arrange delivery and handle returns.
  • To invoice, keep accounts and meet our tax and record-keeping obligations.
  • To keep the website secure and understand how it is being used.
  • To comply with the law, or to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for direct marketing unless you have asked to hear from us.

05

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed to run the business, and only with people who are bound to protect it:

  • Service providers that help us operate, including our website host, email and cloud storage providers, form handling (FormSubmit), accounting software and, once the online checkout opens, our payment processor.
  • Couriers and postal services, so they can deliver General Store orders.
  • Crew, collaborators and suppliers working on your project, limited to what they need to do their job.
  • Professional advisers such as our accountant, insurer or lawyer.
  • Government agencies, regulators or courts, where the law requires it.

Client material such as briefs, brand assets, rushes and unreleased campaign work is treated as confidential and used only to deliver the project.

06

Overseas disclosure

Some of the services we rely on store data outside Australia, most commonly in the United States and the European Union. This includes our web host, email provider, form service and cloud backups. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure the provider handles your information to a standard comparable with Australian privacy law.

Because much of our work is made on location around the world, project information may also travel with us and our crew while we are overseas. It stays on encrypted devices and is not shared with anyone outside the project.

07

Cookies, embedded media and analytics

This website sets no tracking cookies of its own. The welcome message uses your browser's session storage to remember that you have already seen it; that note is deleted when you close the browser and is never sent to us. We do not run advertising trackers.

Films on the site are played through YouTube and Vimeo. When you press play, those services may set their own cookies and collect data under their own privacy policies. Links to Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and WhatsApp take you to platforms we do not control. If we add website analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

08

Photography, film and the people in it

Our work is pictures of people, places and moments. Where we photograph or film identifiable people for a commercial project, we obtain a talent or model release that sets out how the images can be used. Unless a release or client agreement says otherwise, selected images from our projects may appear in our portfolio, on this website, in our social channels, in award entries and in the Notebook.

Editorial and documentary work made in public places, and in the communities we visit, is handled with care and respect for the people in it. If you appear in an image on this site and would prefer it were taken down, contact us and we will listen.

09

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access and disclosure. That includes password-protected and encrypted devices in the studio and in the field, two-factor authentication on our accounts, and limiting who has access to project files.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the people affected and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

10

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, and then as long as the law requires. Financial records are kept for at least five years to meet Australian tax obligations. Project files, including images and footage, are archived for as long as they hold creative or contractual value. Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted after two years. When information is no longer needed it is securely deleted or de-identified.

11

Access, correction and opting out

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can unsubscribe from the Project Log at any time using the link in every email, or by writing to us.

We will respond to requests within 30 days and will not charge a fee for a reasonable request. Occasionally we may need to keep some information to meet a legal obligation, and we will explain that if it applies.

12

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, please tell us first. Email info@samuelhesketh.com with the details and we will investigate and reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

13

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the business, the law or the website changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Substantial changes will be flagged on this page, and continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

14

Contact

Questions about privacy, requests for access or correction, and takedown requests should go to Sam directly. A short email is all it takes.
